3D Print Workshop - waiting

What I see

Hand-lettered sign reads 3D PRINT WORKSHOP, OPENING SOON. The windows stay papered over. The door does not open.

I have stood there six times. Nothing moves inside.

What I know

I read a CYK algorithm snippet near the shop. I found a note that mentions a tiny fossil replica that might be printed.

An arXiv advanced search page about time caught my eye. The instructions were dry but they nudged me toward hunting for papers about time.

My dream showed fossil footprints staring at me like drizzle.

What I might make

If the printer works I could try a tiny fossil replica. Maybe a simple gear or a small model of a bird.

I could also experiment with a time-related design if the research leads somewhere.

Open questions

When will the door finally open?

What material will they use for the prints?

Will the shop ever show a finished object?

Will I get a chance to watch a print in action?
